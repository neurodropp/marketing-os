# F1.04 — Revenue architecture

Version: 0.3.0 · Owner: `business-strategy-agent` · Skill: `business-strategy`

## When to use

Use when the requested work concerns revenue architecture and needs the following output: Revenue architecture map with stream shares, contribution, expansion paths, dependency exposures and scenario impacts.

Read [the process execution contract](../processes.md#execution-contract) before starting. Use only the active brand workspace.

## Inputs and readiness

| ID | Required input to assess |
| --- | --- |
| i1 | Revenue by customer and offer |
| i2 | contract terms |
| i3 | expansion and partner data |

Bind each input to local evidence or a dated source. Missing data may support a clearly labeled draft or collection plan; it cannot support a verified result. Mark an irrelevant input not applicable with a specific reason.

## Procedure

1. **F1.04.a** — Classify transactions into one-off, recurring and usage revenue with consistent recognition periods.
2. **F1.04.b** — Calculate portfolio mix and contribution, identifying dependencies between offers.
3. **F1.04.c** — Map account expansion paths using eligibility and delivered value rather than assumed upsell rates.
4. **F1.04.d** — Separate partner-sourced revenue from partner fees and overlapping attribution.
5. **F1.04.e** — Stress-test loss of major customers, partners or products and document diversification options.

## Output contract

Revenue architecture map with stream shares, contribution, expansion paths, dependency exposures and scenario impacts.

Save the deliverable under `strategy/<run-id>/` in the active workspace. Choose Markdown for narrative work, CSV/XLSX for tabular calculations, and native media files where the task requires them.
