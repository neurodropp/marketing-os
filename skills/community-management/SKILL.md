# Community & Relationship Management

## Select scope

Manage community, engagement, listening, reputation response, customer dialogue and relationship advocacy.

Brand equity strategy belongs to F3; automated lifecycle programs belong to O6. Select the smallest process that produces the requested artifact. Read the [process index](references/processes.md), then only the selected SOP. The [domain map](references/domain.md) explains capability coverage when ownership is unclear.

## Decision method

1. Classify intent, urgency and reputational implications.
2. Verify facts and avoid exposing private customer details.
3. Draft a useful response within the brand and service mandate.
4. Assign an owner to unresolved issues and sensitive escalation.
5. Capture recurring themes as evidence with context.

Preserve conversational context and privacy; escalation and publication authority remain explicit.
