# O3.03 — Paid social operating procedure

Use this reference for campaign setup or recurring paid social operations. Populate thresholds from the approved project plan; no universal spend or performance thresholds are assumed.

## Required context

Approved audience, offer, positioning, brief, assets, destination, budget, conversion definition, CRM handoff and operator mandate. Record the versions used. Identify the channel owner, execution operator, approver and escalation contacts.

## Setup and launch readiness

1. Open a project run and link the approved context.
2. Specify platform objective, audience, placements, campaign structure and naming.
3. Apply approved budget, dates and guardrails.
4. Inspect asset previews, claims, links, calls to action and localization.
5. Test click → destination → conversion → analytics → CRM.
6. Record QA evidence, launch approval and rollback or pause procedure.
7. Launch only if a capable authorized tool is available; otherwise deliver the setup specification. Verify delivery before claiming the campaign is live.

## Daily

Check spend, pacing, disapprovals, destination health and tracking. Compare downstream conversion quality with platform signals, accounting for data delay. Classify anomalies and act within the operator's mandate or open an incident. Record the check, changes and reasons.

## Weekly

Compare audiences, creative, placements, offers and funnel stages. Examine saturation, creative fatigue and message-destination mismatch. Decide maintain, rotate, correct, pause or test. Hand test hypotheses to experimentation and new creative needs to messaging/content owners. Record the next evaluation date.

## Monthly

Reconcile spend with available financial data. Analyze validated outcomes, lead quality, CAC, margin and cohort behavior where measurable. Disclose attribution and missing-data limitations. Distinguish tactical issues from offer, funnel or strategy problems. Propose resource changes to the authorized owner.

## Exceptions

| Signal | Initial response | Handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spend outside mandate | Contain within authorized reversible controls; preserve evidence. | Marketing/business owner |
| Missing or inconsistent tracking | Mark affected data unreliable; pause launch/scaling decisions that depend on it. | Infrastructure and analytics |
| Broken destination | Stop traffic to the broken path within mandate. | Conversion and technical owner |
| Unsupported claim | Withdraw or replace the asset within mandate. | Creative/brand approver |
| Low-quality leads at high volume | Inspect targeting, offer, form and qualification; do not scale on CPL alone. | Research, offer, conversion and analytics |
| Reputation concern | Route the issue with context; avoid improvised public claims. | Community and brand owner |

## Completion evidence

Campaign IDs, approved input versions, budget authorization, QA results, change log, incidents, metric definitions, review decisions and final outcome. Missing results remain explicitly unmeasured.
