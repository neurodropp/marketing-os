# Customer Lifecycle & Monetization

Use the active project's work order and second-brain snapshot. Work only inside that project's scope. If no work order exists, capture the requested outcome, relevant inputs and missing facts before producing a draft. Missing facts are unknown, not permission to invent evidence.

## Method

1. Define the segment, trigger and customer outcome.
2. Check eligibility, suppressions and contact pressure.
3. Match the approved offer and message to the stage.
4. Design normal, exit and support handoff paths.
5. Measure activation, retention or expansion with appropriate uncertainty.

## Contract

Inputs: Customer states, offer eligibility, preferences, events and lifecycle data.

Deliver: Lifecycle program, contact rules, sequence and evaluation plan. Include sources, assumptions, limitations, decision status, and proposed memory updates. A handoff names the next owner and its acceptance criteria.

Boundary: Product and customer success own service delivery. Avoid equating email engagement with retention.
